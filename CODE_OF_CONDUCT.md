# Community Code of Conduct

PYTHON HUB welcomes constructive participation from people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and perspectives.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and decisions rather than people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and assume good faith where reasonable.
- Share context and help make technical discussions accessible.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Include relevant context and links, but avoid redistributing private information. Reports will be reviewed with care and handled as confidentially as practical.

Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that do not meet these standards.
